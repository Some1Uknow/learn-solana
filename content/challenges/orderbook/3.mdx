# Challenge 3: Best Bid And Best Ask

The top of book is the most important summary of an orderbook.

## Task

Implement `best_bid_ask`.

## Rules

- Best bid = highest bid price.
- Best ask = lowest ask price.
- Return `None` when a side has no orders.

Use iteration and comparisons; no matching is needed yet.
