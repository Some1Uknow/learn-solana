# Challenge 8: Insert Limit Order Sorted

A book side must remain sorted after inserting a new limit order.

## Task

Implement `insert_sorted`.

## Rules

- Bid book: highest price first.
- Ask book: lowest price first.

This challenge focuses on maintaining book shape, not matching.
