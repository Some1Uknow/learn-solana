# Challenge 4: Single Fill Calculation

Before matching a full book, handle one maker order.

## Task

Implement `fill_once`.

## Rule

The filled quantity is the smaller of taker quantity and maker quantity.

Return:

```txt
(filled_qty, taker_remaining, maker_remaining)
```

For taker `7` and maker `5`, fill `5`, leaving taker `2` and maker `0`.
